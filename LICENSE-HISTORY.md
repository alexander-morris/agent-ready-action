## Why the change

The action is funded by metered scans through
[mitosislabs.ai/agent-ready](https://mitosislabs.ai/agent-ready). Under MIT,
anyone could take the whole thing and stand up a competing paid service from it
on day one. PolyForm Shield closes exactly that hole and nothing else: it is not
a "source-available, actually proprietary" license, and it puts no restriction on
using the action, in a company, at any scale, for free.
